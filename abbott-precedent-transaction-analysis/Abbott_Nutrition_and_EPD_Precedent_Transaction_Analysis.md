# Abbott — Precedent Transaction Analysis

### Potential Divestiture of the Nutrition and Established Pharmaceuticals (EPD) Businesses

**Prepared:** June 2026 | **Basis:** FY2024 reported segment financials (latest full year audited 10-K) | **Status:** Indicative / discussion draft

> **Purpose & caveat.** This is a precedent-transaction (comparable M&A) valuation screen intended to frame an indicative enterprise value (EV) range for a potential sale or carve-out of Abbott's Nutrition and Established Pharmaceuticals (EPD) segments. Target financials are taken from Abbott's public segment reporting; **segment EBITDA is estimated** (Abbott does not disclose EBITDA by segment) by grossing up disclosed segment operating margin for depreciation & amortization. Figures are indicative and for discussion only — not investment advice or a fairness opinion. A confirmatory analysis would require management-adjusted, carve-out (stand-alone) financials, allocation of stranded/corporate costs, tax structuring, and a data-room–based quality-of-earnings review.

## 1. Executive Summary

| Business | FY24 Sales | Est. FY24 EBITDA* | Selected EV/Sales | Selected EV/EBITDA | **Indicative EV range** | Midpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nutrition** | ~$8.4bn | ~$1.7–1.9bn | 2.0x – 3.0x | 11x – 15x | **~$18bn – $26bn** | ~$22bn |
| **Established Pharmaceuticals (EPD)** | ~$5.2bn | ~$1.3–1.45bn | 2.5x – 3.5x | 11x – 14x | **~$13bn – $19bn** | ~$16bn |
| **Combined** | ~$13.6bn | ~$3.0–3.3bn | — | — | **~$31bn – $45bn** | **~$38bn** |

\* *Estimated. Abbott discloses segment operating margin, not segment EBITDA. EBITDA approximated by adding back assumed D&A of ~3–4% of sales to disclosed segment operating earnings (see §3).*

**Key takeaways**
- Both assets are **scaled, profitable, emerging-market-weighted franchises** that map cleanly onto an active set of M&A precedents — strategic carve-outs are highly feasible.
- **EPD** is the cleaner story: a high-growth (+9.2% FY24), high-margin (23.7% segment operating margin) **branded-generics franchise concentrated in emerging markets**, which sits at the premium end of the branded-generics deal range.
- **Nutrition** is a "two-speed" asset — an attractive, growing **adult/medical nutrition** half (Ensure, Glucerna, PediaSure) and a structurally challenged **pediatric/infant-formula** half (Similac) carrying a meaningful **NEC product-liability litigation overhang**. We apply a discount to the peak strategic infant-formula multiples (Nestlé/Pfizer, Reckitt/Mead Johnson) to reflect this.
- A **sum-of-the-parts** of Nutrition (selling adult nutrition separately at consumer-health multiples, retaining/ring-fencing infant formula and its litigation) could unlock value above the blended range — flagged as optionality in §6.

## 2. Target Overview & Sizing (FY2024)

Abbott reported total net sales of **$41,950m** in FY2024. Segment detail:

| Reportable segment | FY24 net sales | FY24 growth | Segment operating margin | % of total sales |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Established Pharmaceuticals (EPD) | ~$5.19bn | +9.2% | 23.7% | ~12% |
| **Nutritional Products** | ~$8.41bn | +5.9% | 17.9% | ~20% |
| Diagnostic Products | ~$9.34bn | — | — | ~22% |
| Medical Devices | ~$19.0bn | — | — | ~45% |

**Nutrition** — global #1/#2 in infant and adult nutrition; brands include Similac, PediaSure, Ensure, Glucerna, Pedialyte. Roughly split between **Pediatric** nutrition (infant formula, child nutrition) and **Adult / International** nutrition, with adult now the larger and faster-growing half. U.S. infant formula sales recovered in 2023–24 following the 2022 Sturgis plant recall/supply disruption.

**Established Pharmaceuticals (EPD)** — **branded generics sold almost exclusively in emerging markets** (key geographies include India, Russia, China, Brazil, LatAm, SE Asia, MEA). No U.S. branded-generics exposure. This is structurally a *growth* generics business — distinct from mature Western generics — driven by EM volume, rising middle-class healthcare spend, and a broad branded portfolio across GI, CNS, women's health, cardio-metabolic and respiratory.

Abbott does not publish segment EBITDA. We approximate:

| Disclosed segment operating margin | 17.9% | 23.7% |
| Implied segment operating earnings | ~$1.50bn | ~$1.23bn |
| Assumed D&A add-back (% of sales) | ~3.5–4.0% | ~2.5–3.0% |
| **Estimated EBITDA** | **~$1.7–1.9bn** | **~$1.3–1.45bn** |
| Implied EBITDA margin | ~21–22% | ~26–28% |

Nutrition is the more capital-intensive (manufacturing/plants) business, hence a larger D&A add-back. These ranges should be refined against carve-out QoE financials.

## 4. Precedent Transactions — Nutrition (Infant + Adult/Medical Nutrition)

| Date | Acquirer | Target | Enterprise Value | EV / Sales | EV / EBITDA | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2007 | Danone | Numico | ~€12.3bn (~$16.8bn) | ~4.5x | **~22x** | Baby + medical nutrition; peak strategic multiple |
| 2007 | Nestlé | Gerber (from Novartis) | $5.5bn | ~2.8x | ~20x (est.) | Made Nestlé #1 in baby food |
| 2012 | Nestlé | Pfizer Nutrition | $11.85bn | ~5.0x | **~20–24x** | Infant nutrition; EM (China) entry; ~$2.1bn sales / ~$0.5bn EBITDA (2011) |
| 2016/17 | Danone | WhiteWave | $12.5bn | ~2.7x | ~20x (FY16e) | Organic/plant-based; not infant — lower-multiple bookend |
| 2017 | Reckitt Benckiser | Mead Johnson | $17.9bn | ~4.4x (FY15) / 4.9x (FY16) | **17.4x** (≈14x w/ synergies) | Pure-play infant formula; closest single comp |
| 2021 | Nestlé | The Bountiful Company | $5.75bn | ~2.7x | ~17x | Vitamins/supplements (adult-nutrition-adjacent) |

**Reference range (as transacted):** EV/Sales ~2.7x – 5.0x; EV/EBITDA ~17x – 22x.

**Selected range for Abbott Nutrition (discounted): EV/Sales 2.0x – 3.0x; EV/EBITDA 11x – 15x.**

*Rationale for the discount to peak comps:*
- Those 2007–2017 deals were **scarcity-driven, pure-play, high-growth infant-formula** assets bought at the top of the cycle. The infant-formula category has since de-rated on **declining global birth rates** and category maturity.
- Abbott Nutrition is a **blended** asset (≈half adult, half pediatric); the pediatric half carries the **NEC litigation overhang** (see §6) and the legacy of the 2022 Sturgis recall.
- Offsetting positives: global #1/#2 scale, the **fast-growing, high-margin adult nutrition** franchise, and strong EM exposure — which support the *upper* half of the selected range and the SOTP optionality in §6.

| 2014 | Sun Pharma | Ranbaxy | ~$4.0bn (incl. debt) | ~2.2x | n/m (low-margin target) | India/EM generics consolidation |

| 2017 | Bain Capital / Cinven | STADA | €5.32bn | ~2.4x | **13.4x** (FY16) | PE take-private; branded generics + consumer health |
| 2018 | Advent | Zentiva (Sanofi EU generics) | ~€1.9bn | ~2.5x | **~10.3x** | EU/EM branded generics carve-out |
| 2025 | CapVest | STADA (majority stake) | ~€10bn (reported) | ~3x (est.) | **~11.3x** | Latest large branded-generics print; re-rate vs 2017 |

**Listed branded-generics reference multiples (context):** Krka ~11.5x; Hikma ~6.3x; Richter Gedeon ~6x EV/EBITDA — illustrating the gap between premium EM-growth names (~11x) and mature/Western-skewed names (~6x).

**Reference range (transacted):** EV/Sales ~2.2x – 3.6x; EV/EBITDA ~9x – 13.4x.

**Selected range for Abbott EPD (premium positioning): EV/Sales 2.5x – 3.5x; EV/EBITDA 11x – 14x.**

*Rationale:*
- EPD is a **pure emerging-market branded-generics franchise** with **above-peer growth (+9.2%)** and **high margin (23.7% segment operating margin)** — it belongs at the **top** of the branded-generics range (alongside STADA/Krka, well above Hikma/Richter).
- Logical buyers: EM-focused strategics (Sun, Cipla, Aurobindo, Dr. Reddy's, Hikma, Viatris, Sandoz, Aspen) and large-cap PE with healthcare platforms (Advent, Bain, Cinven, CapVest, KKR).
- Watch-items pressuring the lower end: **FX translation risk** (EM-currency revenue), pricing/tender dynamics, and the need to fund continued product registration/BD to sustain growth.

## 6. Key Value Drivers & Risks

**Nutrition**
- 🔻 **NEC product-liability litigation (material overhang).** Cow's-milk-based preterm infant formula (Similac) is the subject of large U.S. jury verdicts — e.g., a **$495m** Missouri verdict (Jul-2024), a **$60m** Illinois verdict (Mar-2025) and a **$58m** Chicago award (Apr-2026) — with several hundred active federal/state cases. Outcomes are volatile (Abbott has also won/been awarded defense verdicts and is appealing). A buyer will demand **indemnities, escrow/holdback, or a litigation carve-out**; unresolved, this is the single biggest discount driver to the pediatric half.
- 🔻 Structural decline in global birth rates pressuring infant-formula volumes.
- 🔺 Fast-growing, high-margin **adult/medical nutrition** (Ensure, Glucerna) — defensive, aging-demographics tailwind; arguably worth **consumer-health multiples (3–4x sales / 15–18x EBITDA)** on a stand-alone basis.
- 🔺 #1/#2 global scale; brand equity; EM growth.

**EPD**
- 🔺 EM secular growth, diversified geography/therapy mix, high margin, asset-light vs. innovative pharma.
- 🔻 EM-currency FX translation; government tender/pricing exposure; reliance on continued in-licensing/BD to refresh the portfolio.
- ⚪ Clean of U.S. branded-generics price-fixing litigation that has weighed on Western generics peers.

**Cross-cutting / carve-out**
- **Stranded costs & TSAs:** separating shared manufacturing, R&D, IT and SG&A will create dis-synergies; stand-alone margins may sit below segment-reported margins until ramped.
- **Tax & structuring:** EM/IP footprint makes structuring (and tax leakage on a sale) a key swing factor on net proceeds.
- **Strategic rationale:** divesting both would refocus Abbott on its higher-multiple **Medical Devices + Diagnostics** core — typically EV/EBITDA-accretive to the remaining group and a credible "good-bank" narrative for equity holders.

## 7. Indicative Valuation Output

**Nutrition**
- EV/Sales: 2.0x–3.0x × $8.4bn → **$16.8bn – $25.2bn**
- EV/EBITDA: 11x–15x × ~$1.8bn → **$19.8bn – $27.0bn**
- **Blended indicative EV: ~$18bn – $26bn (midpoint ~$22bn)**

**Established Pharmaceuticals (EPD)**
- EV/Sales: 2.5x–3.5x × $5.2bn → **$13.0bn – $18.2bn**
- EV/EBITDA: 11x–14x × ~$1.4bn → **$15.4bn – $19.6bn**
- **Blended indicative EV: ~$13bn – $19bn (midpoint ~$16bn)**

**Combined indicative EV: ~$31bn – $45bn (midpoint ~$38bn).**

> Sensitivities worth modelling next: (i) NEC litigation provision / indemnity cost on Nutrition; (ii) Nutrition SOTP (adult at consumer-health multiples vs. pediatric at a litigation-discounted multiple); (iii) stand-alone (carve-out) margin haircut for stranded costs; (iv) EM FX on EPD; (v) tax leakage on disposal structure.
