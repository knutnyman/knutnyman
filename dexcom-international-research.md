# Dexcom (DXCM) — International Business Deep Dive
### Tender Wins, Losses, Pipeline & Competitive Intelligence
*Research compiled: June 5, 2026. Sources: Dexcom IR, SEC filings, earnings transcripts, national health authority documents, industry press.*

---

## Executive Summary

Dexcom's international business (~28% of total revenue, $1.33B in FY2025) is in genuine re-acceleration after a near-disastrous Q2 2024. The growth driver is almost entirely **reimbursement expansion** — France (T2D basal insulin, June 2024), Australia G7 NDSS (March 2025), New Zealand Pharmac (October 2024), and Ontario ODB (July 2025) are the concrete wins behind Q3 2025 +22%, Q4 2025 +18%, and Q1 2026 +26% reported international growth rates.

The single biggest upcoming catalyst globally is the **CONNECT trial readout at ADA, June 6, 2026** — an RCT of CGM in non-insulin Type 2 diabetes patients. A positive result would trigger reimbursement processes in the UK, Germany, France, Australia, and the US simultaneously. The second biggest near-term event is whether Dexcom wins **GKV reimbursement for Dexcom Flex in Germany** for basal-insulin T2D — the May 2026 cash-pay launch was deliberately a market-seeding strategy ahead of a reimbursement application.

**Abbott remains structurally dominant internationally** (~56% global CGM volume share vs. Dexcom's ~35%), with earlier market entry, lower price points, and stronger payer relationships across APAC and emerging markets. No market has run a single-winner exclusive CGM tender that Dexcom formally lost; the reimbursement model is an open formulary in most countries. The competitive gap manifests as market share, not contract exclusions.

## 2. Reimbursement Wins — Country-by-Country

### United Kingdom — NHS England/Scotland/Wales/NI
**Status: Won (open formulary, both Dexcom and Abbott listed)**

- **August 2022**: Dexcom ONE added to NHS Drug Tariff for all T1D and insulin-treated T2D. This was 3+ years after Abbott FreeStyle Libre (listed 2019) — Abbott's structural first-mover advantage.
- **October 2024**: Dexcom ONE+ replaced Dexcom ONE on formulary; ONE phased out by May 2025.
- **April 2026**: Dexcom G6 withdrawn; G7 replaces on NHS scripts.
- **Coverage mechanic**: NICE NG17 guidance (updated 2023) recommends CGM for all T1D and insulin-treated T2D. Both Abbott and Dexcom listed; no exclusive contract. NHS does not publish framework pricing.
- **Market share reality**: Abbott FreeStyle Libre dominates NHS prescriptions, especially for T2D. In NHS Dorset guidance (May 2025), FreeStyle Libre 3 listed as *preferred* for T2D; Dexcom as alternative. Dexcom co-equal for T1D in most formularies.
- **Total UK T1D population**: ~300,000.

### France
**Status: Won — standout recent win, cited as top international growth driver**

- **June 2024**: France becomes **first European country** to reimburse Dexcom CGM for T2D on non-intensive insulin (basal only, HbA1c ≥8%). Product: Dexcom ONE. Eligible population: ~**100,000 T2D patients**. Dexcom IR press release June 4, 2024.
- **September–October 2024**: Dexcom ONE+ added to Social Security reimbursement list (Arrêté du 11 septembre 2024, Légifrance). Extended to both intensive and non-intensive T2D. Available in pharmacies from ~October 7, 2024. Limit: 36 sensors/patient/year. Prescribing requires endocrinologist, paediatrician, or GP.
- France was cited on Q4 2024, Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4 2025, and Q1 2026 earnings calls as the **#1 international market contributor to growth acceleration**.
- **Pending**: Expansion to non-insulin T2D requires HAS (Haute Autorité de Santé) reassessment, likely contingent on CONNECT trial data.

### Germany
**Status: Partial win — insulin-dependent covered; non-insulin T2D gap**

- **2016**: G-BA (Federal Joint Committee) approved GKV (statutory health insurance, >90% of population) reimbursement for CGM for T1D and intensive insulin T2D. Coverage requires CGM with alarms. Triggered by Dexcom's acquisition of distributor Nintamed.
- **2024**: Dexcom secured contract with insurer **KKH** for T2D on Basal Oral Therapy — incremental expansion.
- **May 2026 (announced May 18, 2026)**: Dexcom launches **Dexcom Flex** in Germany for T2D on basal insulin, oral medications, or GLP-1. Launch is **cash-pay / self-funded** — explicitly confirms Dexcom has **not yet won GKV reimbursement** for this population. A deliberate market-seeding strategy ahead of reimbursement application.
- Germany is the #1 upcoming reimbursement catalyst in Europe. Target population: ~6 million non-insulin T2D patients in Germany.
- **Abbott position**: FreeStyle Libre dominates German T2D prescriptions at lower price point through flash CGM (no receiver, factory calibrated). This is the core competitive battle for Dexcom Flex.

### Australia — NDSS (National Diabetes Services Scheme)
**Status: Won — expanded significantly in 2024–25**

- **July 2022**: NDSS expanded CGM access to all T1D adults (previously age-restricted). Dexcom G6 listed.
- **September 2024**: Dexcom G7 commercial launch in Australia.
- **March 1, 2025**: **Dexcom G7 added to NDSS subsidy**. Patient co-pay: AUD $11.60/sensor (10-day wear); max 9 sensors per 3-month transaction. Covers all NDSS-eligible T1D. Confirmed via NDSS.com.au.
- **Non-insulin T2D**: Not covered — CONNECT trial readout (June 2026) is the potential trigger.
- **Stelo OTC**: Late 2026/early 2027 launch planned — bypasses reimbursement pathway for cash-pay market.

### New Zealand — Pharmac
**Status: Won (new win, effective October 2024)**

- **October 1, 2024**: Pharmac began funding CGMs for all New Zealanders with T1D (and some insulin-requiring Type 3c). Prior to this date: **no Dexcom products were publicly subsidised in New Zealand**.
- Products funded: Dexcom ONE+ (standalone T1D), Dexcom G6 and G7 (with insulin pumps).
- Process: Pharmac consultation opened March 2024 → PTAC Diabetes Subcommittee recommendation → October implementation.
- NZ T1D population: ~25,000.
- Cited on Q4 2024 earnings call alongside France as key new access market.

### Japan
**Status: Won (C150 insurance category Dec 2022) — but distribution disruption in 2024**

- **December 2022**: Japanese NHI expanded "C150" insurance category to cover Dexcom G6 for all patients on at least once-daily insulin, regardless of diabetes type. Distributed by **Terumo Corporation**.
- **October 2023**: Terumo announced cessation of Dexcom distribution, effective March 31, 2024, as Dexcom shifted to direct distribution.
- **April 2024 onward**: Dexcom operates directly in Japan. Created commercial disruption through the transition; Q1 2025 cited Japan as a key growth driver as the direct model stabilised.
- **Dexcom G7 in Japan**: PMDA approval status not publicly confirmed as of research date. Abbott FreeStyle Libre has broader prescriber mindshare and has been reimbursed since March 2022. Japan CGM market: USD $295M (2024), forecast $626M by 2033.

### Canada — Ontario + Provincial Programs
**Status: Won (Ontario, major population win July 2025)**

- **July 31, 2025**: Ontario Drug Benefit (ODB) Program listed Dexcom G7 for **all Ontario insulin users**, regardless of diabetes type. Ontario = ~14 million people; largest Canadian province. Cited on Q1 2026 earnings as highlighted access win.
- G6 and G7 also covered under Ontario's Assistive Devices Program (ADP) for T1D.
- **Quebec, Alberta**: Expanded coverage for insulin T2D (details vary by province).
- Remaining provinces (BC, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Atlantic): Incomplete coverage; active lobbying underway.

### Estonia
**Status: Won (January 1, 2025)**

- Estonian Health Insurance Fund (Tervisekassa) extended reimbursement for Dexcom ONE/ONE+ to T2D patients requiring rapid-acting insulin. 90% subsidy; 4 transmitters and/or 40 sensors/year. Small market but signals Eastern European momentum.

### Other European Markets — ONE+ Rollout
- **Spain, Belgium, Netherlands, Poland**: Dexcom ONE+ launched February 2024 as entry product.
- **Dexcom Academy**: Planned rollout to Italy, Poland, Turkey, Sweden throughout 2026 (HCP education/onboarding platform in first five countries including Saudi Arabia).

## 3. Where Dexcom Has NOT Won / Competitive Gaps

### Framework Note
CGM reimbursement in most markets is an **open formulary** (multiple products listed), not a single-winner exclusive tender. There are **no confirmed cases of Dexcom being excluded from a national tender that Abbott or another competitor won exclusively**. The competitive disadvantage manifests as:
- Abbott having significantly higher market share volume in markets where both are listed
- Abbott having been listed earlier (years ahead in UK, Germany)
- Dexcom not yet having reimbursement in entire market segments (non-insulin T2D in most markets, India, Brazil, Mexico entirely)

### Germany — Non-Insulin T2D GKV (Biggest Active Gap)
Dexcom Flex launched cash-pay May 2026 explicitly because GKV does not yet reimburse this population. Abbott's FreeStyle Libre holds dominant share here. This is the single most important unresolved reimbursement battle in Dexcom's largest European market.

### UK — Abbott's Structural NHS Advantage
Abbott listed on NHS Drug Tariff since 2019 vs. Dexcom's 2022 listing. Three-year head start created installed base and prescriber habit. In NHS formulary guidance for T2D specifically, FreeStyle Libre is often listed as first-line; Dexcom as alternative. NHS prescribing data consistently shows Abbott dominant (exact figures not publicly available).

### Japan — Abbott First-Mover + G7 Uncertainty
Abbott FreeStyle Libre reimbursed since March 2022. Dexcom G6 followed December 2022. Dexcom G7 NHI listing status unclear. Abbott has broader prescriber reach and consumer brand awareness. Direct distribution transition (2024) created commercial disruption.

### India — No National Coverage (Future Target)
- No national reimbursement through CGHS or ESIC.
- Abbott launched FreeStyle Libre 2 Plus in India **August 2025** — invested ahead of Dexcom.
- Local player Tracky launched India's first Bluetooth CGM June 2025.
- Dexcom India Operations Pvt Ltd incorporated April 2024 in Bangalore; strategy relies on private pay for now.
- CEO Jake Leach (May 2026 Investor Day): India named as formal 2-year market entry target alongside Brazil and Mexico. Combined intensive insulin population across the three: ~15 million.
- India CGM market: USD $241M (2025).

### Brazil — No Public Reimbursement (Future Target)
- No SUS (public health system) reimbursement for CGM.
- Private ANS-regulated insurance covers CGM for T1D and select insulin T2D.
- High import tariffs, ANVISA registration, cost barriers.
- Both Dexcom and Abbott operate through private/commercial channels only.
- Explicitly cited by CEO as 2-year entry target. Brazil CGM market: USD $307M (2025).

### Mexico — Future Target
- No national reimbursement program.
- Cited with India and Brazil as next 2-year entry market at May 2026 Investor Day.

### China — Minimal Presence
- No confirmed NMPA approval for Dexcom G7.
- Domestic players (Sinocare, Medtrum) hold significant share in price-sensitive segments.
- Abbott has stronger China footprint.
- Not named as near-term priority at Investor Day.
- China CGM market: USD $326M (2026), projected $1.39B by 2033 (23% CAGR) — the largest long-term unaddressed opportunity globally.

### Middle East — Distributor-Led, No National Tender
- No national CGM reimbursement in Saudi Arabia or UAE through government payers.
- NUPCO (Saudi PIF procurement body): No open CGM tender publicly identified.
- Dexcom Academy launched in KSA in early 2026 (HCP education focus).
- Saudi Vision 2030 NCD strategy could trigger pilot formulary work; no timeline.
- MEA CGM market: USD $320M (2025), forecast $855M by 2034 at 11.5% CAGR.

### Southeast Asia & Eastern Europe
- No national reimbursement programs across ASEAN (regulatory approvals fragmentary).
- Eastern Europe (Czech Republic, Hungary, Romania): No GKV-equivalent CGM coverage; Dexcom ONE+ launched in Poland 2024.
- Belarus government issued a CGM tender in August 2025 for Sinocare iCAN i3 (lowest-cost Chinese device) — indicates Eastern European procurement exists but currently captures ultra-low-cost segment.

## 4. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Global CGM Share | Key International Advantage | Markets Where They Clearly Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Abbott FreeStyle Libre** | ~56% revenue / unit share | First-mover in EU NHS systems, lower price (flash CGM, no receiver), 37+ reimbursed countries, factory calibrated | UK (T2D prescriptions), Germany (T2D installed base), Japan (volume), APAC broadly, India, China |
| **Dexcom** | ~35% revenue share | Superior MARD accuracy (8.2% vs 9.2%), AID/pump integration (G7 + Omnipod 5, Tandem), longer proven clinical evidence base | US (premium segment), Australia/NZ, France (gaining), Canada |
| **Medtronic** (Guardian CGM) | ~5-7% | Integrated with Medtronic insulin pumps; institutional hospital relationships | Closed-loop AID in EU/US hospitals; not competitive as standalone CGM |
| **Senseonics** (Eversense) | <2% | 180-day implantable; no daily wear burden | Niche: Germany, some Scandinavian markets; long-wear preference patients |
| **Domestic players** (Sinocare, Medtrum, Tracky) | Growing | Price advantage | China, India, Eastern Europe |

**Abbott–Dexcom patent settlement (December 2024)**: All outstanding global patent litigation settled with 10-year cross-licensing / zero-royalty peace agreement. Removes $500M+ litigation cost overhang; competition now purely on product and reimbursement execution.

**Abbott structural edge in international tenders**: Lower COGS from manufacturing scale enables more aggressive pricing in government tender/formulary environments. FreeStyle Libre's flash CGM design (no separate receiver, no mandatory alarms) was historically preferred by health technology assessment bodies in cost-sensitive markets. Libre now has alarms (FSL2, FSL3) and AID integrations, reducing Dexcom's differentiation.

| Product | Description | US Status | International Timeline |
|---------|------------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Dexcom G7** | 10-day CGM, MARD 8.2% | Current flagship | Launched UK/Germany Oct 2022; most European markets 2023–24; Australia Sept 2024; Japan TBD |
| **Dexcom G7 15-Day** | Extended wear G7 | US commercial launch Dec 1, 2025 | International rollout **H2 2026** |
| **Dexcom ONE+** | Lower-cost CGM for reimbursement-limited markets | N/A (intl only) | Active across Europe (8+ markets); NZ Pharmac-listed |
| **Dexcom Flex** | Purpose-built for T2D basal/non-insulin; new | N/A (intl only) | **Germany launched May 18, 2026** (cash-pay); Europe expansion TBD; GKV reimbursement TBD |

**Product tiering logic** (explicit per management):
- G7/G7 15-Day → markets with broad premium reimbursement (T1D + intensive T2D)
- ONE+ → markets with tiered/limited reimbursement; lower price point
- Flex → markets without T2D non-insulin reimbursement; prescription cash-pay
- Stelo → OTC/wellness; non-insulin T2D without prescribing requirement

## 6. Key Upcoming Catalysts & Tender Pipeline (Next 24 Months)

### Critical (Imminent)

**1. CONNECT Trial — ADA Scientific Sessions, New Orleans, June 6, 2026**
The single most important global catalyst. RCT of Dexcom CGM in ~300 non-insulin T2D patients vs. standard care. If positive:
- **UK**: NICE formal guideline update request likely Q3–Q4 2026; current draft guidance (August 2025) was open for consultation but stopped short of recommending CGM for non-insulin T2D pending evidence
- **Germany**: GKV reimbursement application for Dexcom Flex becomes viable with RCT data
- **France**: HAS reassessment of Dexcom for non-insulin T2D
- **Australia**: NDSS eligibility expansion application
- **US Medicare**: CMS formal petition by Dexcom already filed; CONNECT is the anchor evidence
CEO Jake Leach described this as the potential "DIaMonD/MOBILE equivalent" for non-insulin T2D coverage globally.

**2. Dexcom Flex — Germany GKV Reimbursement Application**
The May 2026 cash-pay launch is stage one of a reimbursement strategy. GKV reimbursement for non-insulin T2D in Germany would be transformational — ~6 million potential patients, the largest European opportunity gap. Timeline: application would follow CONNECT trial data; G-BA assessment typically takes 12–18 months. **No open tender; this is a health technology assessment process.**

### High Priority (H2 2026 – 2027)

**3. G7 15-Day International Rollout (H2 2026)**
New 15-day wear label is a competitive differentiator vs. Abbott in existing reimbursed markets. Dexcom will seek listing at existing reimbursement terms. Some markets may require new formulary submissions (particularly Pharmac NZ, NHS UK).

**4. Stelo International OTC Launch — Australia, UK, South Korea, New Zealand (Late 2026/Early 2027)**
Opens non-insulin T2D market without requiring reimbursement approval. UK requires MHRA clearance; Australia requires TGA approval for OTC classification. This is a bypass strategy for markets where reimbursement for non-insulin T2D is years away.

**5. UK NICE Final Guideline on T2D CGM**
NICE draft guidance (August 2025) was under consultation. A final positive NICE guideline for non-insulin T2D (likely contingent on CONNECT data) would mandate NHS funding — this is the largest single potential coverage expansion in European history by population. UK T2D population: ~4.5 million.

**6. Japan — Dexcom G7 PMDA Approval & NHI Listing**
NHI fee schedule revisions occur every 2 years; the April 2026 cycle has recently completed. A G7 PMDA filing, if submitted in 2025, could enable NHI listing in the next cycle. Japan is the third-largest CGM market in Asia after China. No public filing confirmation as of research date.

**7. Canada — Remaining Provincial Programs**
BC, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Atlantic provinces still lack full Dexcom G7 coverage. Provincial drug benefit decisions are rolling; not structured tenders.

**8. India / Brazil / Mexico — Market Entry (2027–2028)**
CEO-confirmed 2-year targets (from May 2026 Investor Day). Regulatory registration (CDSCO/ANVISA) must precede any reimbursement. Initial strategy: private pay channels; SUS/government tender is years away in Brazil. These are opportunity signals, not near-term tender catalysts.

### Medium-Term Risk

**9. US Medicare DMEPOS Competitive Bidding (Effective January 1, 2028)**
CMS finalised inclusion of CGMs in the Competitive Bidding Program (November 2025). Implementation: ~10 national suppliers selected; effective January 2028. Compresses Medicare pricing. **Downstream risk**: international payers (especially European HTA bodies negotiating under CEPS, GKV, NICE reference pricing frameworks) may use US Medicare contract prices as a reference benchmark — potential pricing headwind for Dexcom across all markets. Both Dexcom and Abbott have actively lobbied against; 950,000+ public comments filed.

## 8. Gross Margin Context

Dexcom does not break out gross margin by geography. Consolidated figures:

| Period | GAAP Gross Margin | Notes |
|--------|------------------|-------|
| FY2024 | 60.5% | Supply chain issues + Q2 disruption |
| FY2025 | ~60.5% | Flat; international mix + rebate pressure |
| Q1 2026 | 63.5% | Improvement from volume leverage + G7 15-Day mix |

International markets structurally carry lower gross margins than US:
- Regulated/tendered pricing in European NHS systems (lower ASP than US commercial)
- ONE+ and Flex are priced below G7
- Rebate structures with national payers
- Industry norm: international tendered medtech margins typically 10–15 points below US commercial equivalents
The Q1 2026 improvement to 63.5% is an encouraging inflection; driven by G7 15-Day ramp and volume leverage rather than international mix improvement.
