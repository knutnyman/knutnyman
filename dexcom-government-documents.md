**URL:** https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng17
**Full guideline PDF:** https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng17/resources/type-1-diabetes-in-adults-diagnosis-and-management-1837276469701
**Recommendations chapter:** https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng17/chapter/recommendations
**Last major update:** August 2022 (minor update August 2024)
**Quality Standard QS208:** https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs208/chapter/Quality-statement-2-Continuous-glucose-monitoring

**Key CGM recommendations (August 2022 — landmark shift):**
- Offer rtCGM **or** isCGM to **ALL adults with type 1 diabetes** — universal access, no restrictive criteria
- Where multiple devices meet patient needs and preferences, offer the device with the **lowest cost**
- Providers must address inequalities in CGM access; monitor uptake by population group
- CGM must be provided with team expertise in its use

### NICE Guideline GID-NG10336 — Type 2 Diabetes CGM (In Development)

**Status page:** https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/indevelopment/gid-ng10336
**Draft guideline (August 2025):** https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/gid-ng10336/documents/450
**Planned final publication:** 18 February 2026 (partial update to NICE NG28)

**Key CGM recommendations in August 2025 draft:**
- Offer CGM to adults with T2D on **multiple daily insulin injections** if they have: recurrent/severe hypoglycaemia; impaired hypoglycaemia awareness; would need ≥8 SMBG tests/day; or a condition preventing capillary testing
- Consider rtCGM as alternative to isCGM if available at same or lower cost
- **Non-insulin T2D: NOT recommended** in this draft — CONNECT trial data awaited

## EU-WIDE PROCUREMENT (TED — Tenders Electronic Daily)

**Key structural finding:** CGM in EU member states is predominantly dispensed through **retail pharmacy under social insurance reimbursement** (France: LPPR/LPP system; Germany: GKV/Hilfsmittelverzeichnis; Netherlands: ZVW). There are **no large EU-wide exclusive CGM tenders** on TED — products do not flow through hospital bulk procurement above EU notice thresholds.

**One identified TED notice:**
- Notice 28043-2026 (France, 15 Jan 2026): https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/28043-2026
- Groupement de commandes régional de Bretagne — broad medical consumables procurement; not CGM-specific

**Germany procurement mechanism:** Dexcom holds **bilateral supply agreements (Versorgungsverträge)** with individual Krankenkassen under §127 SGB V (Social Code Book V). These are not public tenders — they are bilateral negotiations not advertised on TED or public portals. No contract values are disclosed. The GKV Spitzenverband sets product listing criteria via the Hilfsmittelverzeichnis (aids directory).

### G-BA Foundational Decision — rtCGM Reimbursement

**G-BA Beschluss 2623 (foundational CGM decision)**
- **URL:** https://www.g-ba.de/beschluesse/2623/
- **PDF:** https://www.g-ba.de/downloads/39-261-2623/2016-06-16_MVV-RL_rtCGM_BAnz.pdf
- **Title:** "Richtlinie Methoden vertragsärztliche Versorgung (MVV-RL): Kontinuierliche interstitielle Glukosemessung mit Real-Time-Messgeräten (rtCGM) zur Therapiesteuerung bei insulinpflichtigem Diabetes mellitus"
- **Date:** 16 June 2016; **effective 7 September 2016**
- All g-ba.de URLs return HTTP 403 to automated fetchers

**Legal basis:** §135 Abs. 1 SGB V
**Mechanism:** Established rtCGM as a statutory GKV benefit under the MVV-RL (Richtlinie Methoden vertragsärztliche Versorgung)

**Eligibility (as established in 2016 Beschluss):**
- Patients with insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus on intensive conventional therapy (ICT) or insulin pump (CSII)
- Who cannot achieve individually set metabolic control goals despite considering life circumstances
- AND who cannot detect hypoglycaemia in time
- Prescription follows Hilfsmittel-Richtlinie (HilfsM-RL), Section 20 of MVV-RL

### MSAC Assessments — Critical for T2D Expansion Outlook

#### MSAC 1785 — Dexcom ONE for Insulin-Dependent T2D (July 2025)
- **URL:** https://www.msac.gov.au/applications/1785
- **Public Summary Document:** https://www.msac.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-10/1785_final_psd_-_july_2025_-_redacted.pdf
- **Decision date:** July 2025
- **Outcome: DEFERRED — not supported for NDSS listing**
- **Reason:** Clinical claim that CGM leads to overall improvement in glycaemic control (HbA1c) in insulin-dependent T2D was not fully supported — trials were small, short-term, and did not report end-organ outcomes; HbA1c reduction was below the threshold considered meaningful by PBAC and regulatory agencies
- **Implication:** NDSS expansion to insulin-dependent T2D is NOT imminent in Australia; CONNECT trial data and larger RCTs are needed before MSAC would reconsider
