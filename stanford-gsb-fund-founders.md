# Notable Fund Launches Founded by Stanford GSB Alumni

A curated, sourced list of notable venture capital, growth, and private-equity
funds whose **founders are Stanford Graduate School of Business (GSB) alumni**.

*Compiled June 2026. Each entry has been checked to confirm the founder
actually attended/earned a degree from Stanford GSB (an MBA unless noted) —
not merely Stanford undergrad/graduate engineering or law. Edge cases and
common misconceptions are flagged in the appendix.*

---

## Tier 1 — Marquee / Large-Scale Funds

### Khosla Ventures — Vinod Khosla
- **GSB:** MBA, 1980
- **Founded:** 2004 (Menlo Park, CA)
- **Focus:** Early- and growth-stage; AI, clean energy, deep tech, digital health, fintech. Famous "hard tech" and contrarian bets.
- **Scale:** ~$16–18B AUM (2025–26); closed ~$3.1B across three new funds in Nov 2023.
- **Notes:** Khosla previously co-founded Sun Microsystems (1982) and was a longtime Kleiner Perkins partner before launching his own firm. Early backer of OpenAI, Stripe, DoorDash, Instacart, Square.

### Thoma Bravo — Orlando Bravo *(Private Equity)*
- **GSB:** MBA + Stanford Law JD (B.S. economics/political science, Brown)
- **Founded:** Bravo built and rebranded the firm (formerly Thoma Cressey) into Thoma Bravo in 2008; co-founder & managing partner.
- **Focus:** Enterprise software & technology-enabled services buyouts. Dubbed "the King of SaaS" by the *Financial Times*.
- **Scale:** One of the largest software-focused PE firms in the world (well over $130B AUM).
- **Notes:** Not venture capital, but a landmark fund franchise built by a GSB alum.

### Lightspeed Venture Partners — Ravi Mhatre & Barry Eggers (co-founders)
- **GSB:** Mhatre — MBA (plus Stanford BS EE / BA economics); Eggers — MBA (UCLA undergrad). *Both* founding partners are GSB alumni. (Other co-founders: Christopher Schaepe, Peter Nieh.)
- **Founded:** 2000
- **Focus:** Multi-stage, global (enterprise, consumer, fintech, crypto, health). Spun out of Weiss, Peck & Greer.
- **Scale:** One of the world's largest VC platforms (tens of billions AUM). Investments include Snap, Nutanix, Affirm, Rubrik, Mistral.

## Tier 2 — Influential VC Firms Founded by GSB Alumni

### Kaszek Ventures — Hernán Kazah & Nicolás Szekasy (co-founders)
- **GSB:** Both earned Stanford MBAs (Szekasy, MBA 1991). The firm's name combines **Kaz**ah + **Sz**ekasy.
- **Founded:** 2011
- **Focus:** Latin America's leading early-stage VC firm.
- **Scale:** Raised upwards of $2B+; 100+ companies, ~16 of the region's unicorns. Both founders previously built **MercadoLibre** (COO/CFO).
- **Notes:** Seed backer of Nubank — one of the most valuable startups in Latin America.

### Future Ventures — Steve Jurvetson (co-founder)
- **GSB:** MBA (plus Stanford BS EE #1 in class, and MSEE)
- **Founded:** April 2018, with Maryanna Saenko
- **Focus:** Frontier/deep tech — space, AI, synthetic biology, transportation.
- **Notes:** Jurvetson was previously the "J" in **DFJ (Draper Fisher Jurvetson)**; early board member/investor in SpaceX and Tesla.

### Aspect Ventures / Acrew Capital — Theresia Gouw
- **GSB:** MBA (ScB engineering, Brown)
- **Founded:** Aspect Ventures, 2014 (co-founded with Jennifer Fonstad); later co-founded **Acrew Capital** (2019).
- **Focus:** Early-stage; cybersecurity, fintech, enterprise & consumer software.
- **Notes:** One of the most prominent women in VC; teaches a VC course at Stanford GSB. Early investor in ForeScout, Exabeam, Cato Networks. *(Co-founder Jennifer Fonstad is a Harvard MBA — see appendix.)*

### Fifth Wall — Brendan Wallace (co-founder & CEO)
- **GSB:** MBA, 2010 (BA, Princeton)
- **Founded:** 2016 (with Brad Greiwe)
- **Focus:** The largest VC firm focused on **PropTech** (real-estate / built-world technology) and climate tech for real estate.
- **Scale:** Billions in AUM; backed by major real-estate strategics.

### Costanoa Ventures — Greg Sands
- **GSB:** MBA (BA, Harvard)
- **Founded:** 2012
- **Focus:** Early-stage B2B — applied AI, data, security, fintech, vertical SaaS.
- **Notes:** Sands was Netscape's first product manager (and coined the name "Netscape"); previously a managing director at Sutter Hill Ventures.

## Tier 3 — Notable Seed & Emerging-Manager Funds

### Baseline Ventures — Steve Anderson
- **GSB:** MBA (prev. Kleiner Perkins, Microsoft, eBay, Starbucks)
- **Founded:** 2006
- **Focus:** Seed-stage, deliberately small and solo-GP.
- **Notes:** **First seed investor in Instagram**; early investor in Twitter, Heroku, SocialCode. Called by *Forbes* "one of Silicon Valley's most successful — and smallest — investment firms."

### Homebrew — Hunter Walk (co-founder)
- **GSB:** MBA (prev. product leader at YouTube/Google and Linden Lab)
- **Founded:** 2013, with Satya Patel
- **Focus:** Originally classic seed; later moved to an evergreen/permanent-capital model. Co-launched **Screendoor** to back underrepresented emerging fund managers.
- **Notes:** *(Co-founder Satya Patel is a Penn alum — not GSB.)*

### Precursor Ventures — Charles Hudson
- **GSB:** MBA (BA economics & Spanish, Stanford)
- **Founded:** 2015
- **Focus:** Pre-seed / "first institutional check" specialist; one of the most active and diversity-forward pre-seed firms.
- **Notes:** Hudson is also a lecturer at Stanford GSB; previously a partner at SoftTech VC (now Uncork Capital).

### Renegade Partners — Renata Quintini (co-founder)
- **GSB:** MBA + LL.M. (Law, Science & Technology); JD from Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro
- **Founded:** 2019, with Roseanne Wincek
- **Focus:** "Supercritical" Series A/B stage. Quintini previously helped build Felicis Ventures and was a partner at Lux Capital.
- **Notes:** Early-stage investments include Warby Parker, Planet, Cruise Automation, Dollar Shave Club.

### Freestyle Capital — Jenny Lefcourt (General Partner)
- **GSB:** Attended Stanford GSB (BS economics, Wharton) — **left the MBA program** to co-found WeddingChannel.com with classmate Jessica Herrin.
- **Role/Founded:** Promoted to General Partner of Freestyle in 2015 (firm founded 2009 by Dave Samuel & Josh Felser).
- **Focus:** Seed-stage software.
- **Notes:** Included as a prominent GSB-affiliated fund leader; she is a GSB attendee rather than a degree-holder, and is a partner rather than the original founder of Freestyle.

## Tier 4 — GSB Community & Student/Alumni-Organized Funds

### The 20|20 Fund — Steph Mui & GSB Class of 2020
- **GSB:** MBA Class of 2020
- **Founded:** 2020
- **Focus:** Student-led fund created to democratize startup investing; inaugural fund raised $1.5M+ and invested in 16 portfolio companies founded by GSB classmates.
- **Notes:** Designed explicitly to counter perceived elitism/gatekeeping in venture.

### Reaction — Dan Matthies & Enrico Carbone (founding partners)
- **GSB:** Stanford Executive Program (SEP) alumni network
- **Founded:** A global VC fund formed by 150+ Stanford SEP/alumni across 45 countries.
- **Focus:** Global, mission-driven ("transform 1 billion lives in 10 years").
- **Notes:** Built around the Stanford executive-education alumni community rather than the MBA program.

## Appendix — Common Misconceptions / Non-Qualifiers

These prominent investors are frequently *assumed* to be Stanford GSB alumni but
are **not** (so their funds were excluded from the main list):

- **Mary Meeker — Bond Capital** (founded 2019, $1.25B debut). MBA from **Cornell** (Johnson), undergrad DePauw. *Not GSB.*
- **Mike Maples Jr. — Floodgate** (founded 2006). Stanford *undergrad* engineering, but MBA from **Harvard**. *Not GSB.*
- **Jennifer Fonstad — Aspect Ventures co-founder.** MBA from **Harvard**, not GSB.
- **Satya Patel — Homebrew co-founder.** University of Pennsylvania, no GSB.
- **Roelof Botha — Sequoia Capital.** GSB MBA (2000) — *but he leads Sequoia; he did not found it* (Don Valentine founded Sequoia in 1972). A genuine GSB alum, just not a fund *founder*, so listed here for accuracy.

> **Methodology note:** "Stanford GSB" is interpreted as a degree from or
> enrollment in the Stanford Graduate School of Business (MBA, MSx, PhD, or
> executive programs) — distinct from Stanford's undergraduate, engineering,
> or law programs. Where a co-founding team is mixed, only the GSB-affiliated
> founder(s) are credited and the others are flagged.
